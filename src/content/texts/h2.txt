@lesson id=h2-01 level=2 type=dialogue topic=Health minutes=3 source=Original
@title 我不舒服
@titleen I Don't Feel Well
@summary A student visits the doctor with a cold.
@speakers 医生 小李
医生: 你好，你哪里不舒服？ // Hello, where do you not feel well?
小李: 我头疼，还有点儿发烧。 // I have a headache and a bit of a fever.
医生: 你咳嗽吗？ // Do you cough?
小李: 咳嗽。昨天晚上我没睡好。 // Yes. I didn't sleep well last night.
医生: 让我看看。你的嗓子有点儿红。 // Let me take a look. Your throat is a bit red.
小李: 是感冒了吗？ // Is it a cold?
医生: 对，你感冒了。这是药，一天吃三次，每次两片。 // Yes, you have a cold. This is medicine. Take it three times a day, two tablets each time.
小李: 我可以去上课吗？ // Can I go to class?
医生: 最好在家休息两天，多喝水。 // It's best to rest at home for two days and drink more water.
小李: 好的，谢谢医生。 // All right, thank you, doctor.
G: 一天吃三次 ~ Time words and counts tell how often you do something: 一天 + verb + number + 次. ~ 我一天喝三杯水。 ~ I drink three glasses of water a day. ~ 次
G: 最好 ~ 最好 means "had better" and is used to give a suggestion. ~ 你最好早点儿睡。 ~ You had better go to bed early. ~ 最好
Q: What is wrong with Xiaoli? ~ A cold ~ A stomachache ~ A broken leg ~ A toothache
Q: How often should he take the medicine? ~ Three times a day ~ Once a day ~ Twice a week ~ Every hour
Q: What does the doctor advise? ~ Rest at home and drink water ~ Go to class ~ Run every day ~ Eat spicy food
