@lesson id=c2-01 level=6 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Zhang Ji (public domain)
@title 枫桥夜泊
@titleen Night Mooring at Maple Bridge
@summary Zhang Ji, sleepless on a boat, hears the temple bell across the water.
月落乌{乌=crow}啼霜满天， // The moon sets, crows cry, frost fills the sky;
江枫渔火对{对=to face}愁眠。 // river maples and fishing lights face my troubled sleep.
姑苏城外寒山寺， // Outside Gusu city stands Hanshan Temple;
夜半钟{钟=bell}声到客船。 // its midnight bell reaches the traveller's boat.
Q: What fills the sky? ~ Frost ~ Clouds ~ Smoke ~ Stars
Q: What does the traveller hear? ~ A temple bell ~ A flute ~ Rain ~ Drums
Q: Where is he? ~ On a boat ~ At home ~ In a temple ~ On a mountain

@lesson id=c2-02 level=6 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Li Bai (public domain)
@title 黄鹤楼送孟浩然之广陵
@titleen Seeing Meng Haoran Off at Yellow Crane Tower
@summary Li Bai watches his friend's sail disappear down the great river.
故人西辞{辞=to take leave of}黄鹤楼， // My old friend takes leave of Yellow Crane Tower to the west;

孤帆远影碧空尽， // The lone sail's distant shadow vanishes into blue sky;
唯见长江天际流。 // I see only the Yangtze flowing to the horizon.
Q: Where is the friend going? ~ Yangzhou ~ Beijing ~ Chang'an ~ Sichuan
Q: What vanishes into the sky? ~ The lone sail ~ A bird ~ A cloud ~ The moon
Q: What does the poet see at the end? ~ The Yangtze flowing ~ His friend ~ A crane ~ Rain

@lesson id=c2-03 level=6 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Meng Jiao (public domain)
@title 游子吟
@titleen Song of the Wandering Son
@summary A mother sews a coat for her departing son.
慈母手中线， // The thread in a loving mother's hand,
游子身上衣。 // the coat on the wanderer's back.
临{临=on the point of}行{行=departure}密密缝， // Before he leaves she sews it close and tight,
意{意=intent}恐迟迟归。 // fearing he will be slow to return.
谁言寸草心， // Who says the heart of a small blade of grass
报{报=to repay}得{得=to be able}三春晖。 // can repay the spring sun's light?
Q: What is the mother doing? ~ Sewing a coat ~ Cooking ~ Writing ~ Praying
Q: Why does she sew so tightly? ~ She fears he will return late ~ The coat is thin ~ It is cold ~ He asked her to
Q: What does the grass stand for? ~ A child's small heart ~ Spring ~ The mother ~ The road

霜叶红于{于=than}二月花。 // frost-touched leaves are redder than spring flowers.
Q: Why does the poet stop? ~ He loves the maple grove ~ He is tired ~ It rains ~ He is lost
Q: What are redder than spring flowers? ~ Frosted leaves ~ Sunsets ~ Lanterns ~ Cheeks
Q: Where are the homes? ~ Where clouds rise ~ By the river ~ In the valley ~ In the grove

@lesson id=c2-05 level=7 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=2 source=Tang poem by Du Fu (public domain)
@title 春望
@titleen Spring View
@summary Du Fu, in a ruined capital, sees spring return to a broken land.
国破山河在， // The state is broken, yet mountains and rivers remain;
城春草木深。 // spring in the city — grass and trees grow thick.
感时花溅泪， // Moved by the times, flowers splash me with tears;
恨别鸟惊心。 // hating parting, birds startle my heart.
烽火连三月{三月=three months}， // Beacon fires have lasted three months;
家书抵万金。 // a letter from home is worth ten thousand gold.
白头搔更短， // I scratch my white hair and it grows shorter,
浑{浑=almost}欲不胜簪。 // hardly able to hold a hairpin.
Q: What remains although the state is broken? ~ Mountains and rivers ~ The palace ~ The army ~ The poets
Q: What is a letter from home worth? ~ Ten thousand gold ~ Little ~ A horse ~ A house
Q: What is happening to his hair? ~ It is thinning ~ It is dyed ~ It is growing ~ It is cut

@lesson id=c2-06 level=7 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=2 source=Han poem by Cao Cao (public domain)
@title 观沧海
@titleen Gazing at the Vast Sea
@summary Cao Cao stands on a cliff and imagines the sea holding sun, moon and stars.
东临碣石，以观沧海。 // Eastward I reach Jieshi to gaze at the vast sea.
水何澹澹，山岛竦峙。 // How the waters ripple, the island peaks rise tall.
树木丛生，百草丰茂。 // Trees grow in clusters, a hundred grasses flourish.
秋风萧瑟，洪波涌起。 // Autumn wind sighs; great waves surge up.
日月之行{行=course}，若出其中； // The sun and moon in their courses seem to rise from within it;
星汉灿烂，若出其里{里=inside}。 // the bright Milky Way seems to come from inside it.
幸甚至哉，歌以咏志。 // How fortunate indeed — I sing to express my aim.
Q: What does the poet gaze at? ~ The sea ~ A mountain ~ A river ~ A desert
Q: What season is it? ~ Autumn ~ Spring ~ Winter ~ Summer
Q: What seems to come from the sea? ~ Sun, moon and stars ~ Ships ~ Fish ~ Mist

@lesson id=c2-07 level=6 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Song poem by Su Shi (public domain)
@title 题西林壁
@titleen Written on the Wall at Xilin Temple
@summary Su Shi on why we cannot see the mountain while standing in it.
横看/成岭侧成峰， // Seen across it is a ridge, from the side a peak;
远近高低各不同。 // far, near, high, low — each view differs.
不识/庐山/真面目， // I cannot know Lushan's true face,
只{只=only}缘{缘=because of}身{身=oneself}在此山中。 // only because I am within this mountain.
Q: What does the poet say about views? ~ Each angle looks different ~ All are the same ~ Only one is true ~ They are hidden
Q: Why can't he know the true face? ~ He is inside the mountain ~ It is foggy ~ He is blind ~ It is night
Q: What lesson does the poem suggest? ~ Step back to see clearly ~ Climb higher ~ Walk faster ~ Ask a guide

@lesson id=c2-13 level=9 type=classic topic=Philosophy minutes=3 source=Zhuangzi (public domain)
@title 庄子·庄周梦蝶
@titleen Zhuangzi: Zhuang Zhou Dreams of a Butterfly
@summary Was the dreamer a man dreaming a butterfly, or a butterfly dreaming a man?

自喻/适志/与[yu2]{与=(question particle)}！不知周也。 // content with itself, it did not know it was Zhou.

不知周之梦为[wei2]{为=to become}胡蝶与[yu2]{与=(question particle)}，胡蝶之梦为[wei2]{为=to become}周与[yu2]{与=(question particle)}？ // He knew not whether Zhou had dreamed the butterfly, or the butterfly dreamed Zhou.

@lesson id=c2-14 level=9 type=classic topic=Education minutes=3 source=Han Yu, Shi Shuo (public domain)
@title 师说（节选）
@titleen On Teachers (excerpt)
@summary Han Yu defines what a teacher is for.
古之学者必有师。 // Scholars of old always had teachers.
师者，所以传道受业解惑也。 // A teacher is one who transmits the Way, imparts learning and resolves doubts.
人非生而知之者，孰能无惑？ // People are not born knowing — who can be without doubt?
惑而不从师，其为[wei2]{为=to be}惑也，终不解矣。 // If one has doubts but does not follow a teacher, those doubts will never be resolved.
Q: What does a teacher do? ~ Transmit the Way and resolve doubts ~ Grade exams ~ Collect fees ~ Give orders
Q: Who is born knowing? ~ No one ~ Everyone ~ Emperors ~ Sages
Q: What happens without a teacher? ~ Doubts remain unresolved ~ One becomes wise ~ Study is easier ~ Nothing changes

@lesson id=c2-15 level=9 type=classic topic=Ethics minutes=3 source=Fan Zhongyan, Yueyang Lou Ji (public domain)
@title 岳阳楼记（节选）
@titleen Record of Yueyang Tower (excerpt)
@summary Fan Zhongyan's famous statement of public duty.
不以物喜，不以己悲。 // Not delighted by outward things, not saddened by one's own lot.
居庙堂之高，则忧其民； // High in the court, one worries for the people;
处[chu3]{处=to dwell}江湖之远，则忧其君{君=ruler}。 // far away on rivers and lakes, one worries for the ruler.
是进亦忧，退亦忧。 // So advancing brings worry, and retreating brings worry too.
然则何时而乐耶？ // Then when is one happy?
其必曰：先{先=before}天下之忧而忧，后{后=after}天下之乐而乐欤！ // Surely one will say: be first to worry about the world's worries, and last to enjoy the world's joys!
Q: Who does one worry for when at court? ~ The people ~ The ruler ~ Oneself ~ Family
Q: What is the closing maxim about? ~ Worrying before the world and enjoying after ~ Seeking wealth ~ Retiring ~ Travelling
Q: What should one not be moved by? ~ Outward things ~ Poetry ~ Friends ~ Parents

Q: Where does the lotus grow from? ~ Mud ~ Sand ~ Rock ~ Snow
