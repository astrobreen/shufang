@title 汉字的演变
@titleen The Evolution of Chinese Characters
@summary From oracle bones to simplified characters.
汉字是世界上历史最悠久的文字之一，已有三千多年的历史。 // Chinese characters are among the world's oldest scripts, with a history of over three thousand years.
目前发现的最早成熟文字是商朝的甲骨文，因为它是刻在龟甲和兽骨上的。 // The earliest mature writing so far discovered is the Shang dynasty oracle-bone script, so called because it was carved on tortoise shells and animal bones.
后来，文字依次演变为[wei2]金文、大篆和小篆。 // Later the script developed in turn into bronze script, great seal and small seal.
秦始皇统一中国以后，下令统一文字，使小篆成为标准字体。 // After Qin Shi Huang unified China, he ordered the script unified and made small seal the standard.
到了汉代，隶书逐渐流行，书写变得更加方便，汉字的形状也由圆转方。 // By the Han dynasty clerical script gradually became popular, writing became more convenient, and the shape of characters changed from round to square.
之后出现的楷书一直沿用至今，成为现代汉字的基础。 // The regular script that appeared afterwards has been used until today and became the basis of modern characters.
二十世纪五十年代，中国大陆推行简化字，减少了笔画，方便了学习和使用。 // In the 1950s mainland China promoted simplified characters, reducing strokes and making them easier to learn and use.
而台湾、香港等地至今仍然使用繁体字。 // Taiwan, Hong Kong and some other places still use traditional characters today.
