@lesson id=c1-01 level=5 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Li Bai (public domain)
@title 静夜思
@titleen Quiet Night Thought
@summary Li Bai, lying awake in the moonlight, thinks of his hometown.
床前明月光， // Before my bed, the bright moonlight,
疑是地/上[shang4]霜。 // I wonder if it is frost upon the ground.
举头望{望=to gaze at}明月， // I raise my head to gaze at the bright moon,
低头思故乡。 // I lower my head and think of home.
Q: What does the poet see before his bed? ~ Moonlight ~ Snow ~ A candle ~ A river
Q: What does the poet mistake the light for? ~ Frost on the ground ~ Water ~ Silver ~ Snow on a roof
Q: What does he feel at the end? ~ He misses his hometown ~ He is angry ~ He is sleepy ~ He is afraid

@lesson id=c1-02 level=5 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Meng Haoran (public domain)
@title 春晓
@titleen Spring Dawn
@summary Meng Haoran wakes to birdsong and wonders how many blossoms fell in the night rain.
春眠不觉晓， // Sleeping in spring, I don't notice the dawn;
处[chu4]{处=place}处[chu4]{处=place}闻啼鸟。 // everywhere I hear birds singing.
夜来风雨声， // Last night came the sound of wind and rain;
花落知多少？ // how many flowers fell, who knows?
Q: What does the poet not notice? ~ The dawn ~ The wind ~ The time of day ~ The cold
Q: What does he hear everywhere? ~ Birds singing ~ Children playing ~ Temple bells ~ A river
Q: What does he wonder at the end? ~ How many flowers fell ~ When spring will end ~ Where the birds went ~ Who is at the door

Q: What advice does the last line give? ~ Climb higher for a wider view ~ Go home ~ Rest more ~ Sit by the river

@lesson id=c1-04 level=5 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Li Shen (public domain)
@title 悯农
@titleen Pity the Farmers
@summary Li Shen reminds us how hard farmers work for each grain of rice.
锄禾日当午， // Hoeing the seedlings at noon,
汗滴禾下土。 // sweat drips onto the soil below.
谁知/盘/中/餐， // Who knows that the food in our bowls,
粒粒皆辛苦？ // every single grain, comes from hard labour?
Q: Who is the poem about? ~ Farmers ~ Soldiers ~ Merchants ~ Scholars
Q: When are they working? ~ At noon ~ At dawn ~ At night ~ At sunset
Q: What is the message? ~ Don't waste food ~ Work less ~ Grow more rice ~ Buy more bowls

@title 相思

Q: What do red beans stand for? ~ Longing for someone ~ Wealth ~ Good health ~ Courage
Q: What does the poet hope? ~ That you will gather many ~ That you will plant them ~ That you will eat them ~ That you will sell them

@lesson id=c1-08 level=5 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Tang poem by Li Bai (public domain)
@title 赠汪伦
@titleen To Wang Lun
@summary Li Bai thanks a friend who sings a farewell on the shore.
李白乘舟将欲行， // Li Bai is about to leave by boat,

Q: How is Li Bai leaving? ~ By boat ~ By horse ~ On foot ~ By cart
Q: What does he hear? ~ Singing on the bank ~ A bell ~ A flute ~ Rain
Q: What is compared with the pool? ~ Wang Lun's friendship ~ The sea ~ The sky ~ The moon

@lesson id=c1-14 level=8 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=2 source=Song poem by Su Shi (public domain)
@title 水调歌头（节选）
@titleen Prelude to Water Melody (excerpt)
@summary Su Shi, on Mid-Autumn night, wonders about the moon and longs for his brother.
明月几时有？ // When did the bright moon first appear?
把酒问青天。 // Cup in hand, I ask the blue sky.
不知天上宫阙， // I do not know, in the palaces of heaven,
今夕是何年。 // what year it is tonight.
我欲乘风归去， // I wish to ride the wind and return there,
又恐琼楼玉宇， // yet I fear those jade towers and crystal halls
高处/不胜/寒。 // are too cold to bear up high.
起舞弄清影， // I rise and dance with my clear shadow;
何似在人间？ // how can that compare with being among men?
但愿人长久， // I only wish that we may live long,
千里共婵娟。 // and share the beauty of the moon, though a thousand miles apart.
Q: When was the poem written? ~ On Mid-Autumn night ~ At the Spring Festival ~ On a snowy morning ~ At sunrise
Q: What does the poet fear in heaven? ~ It is too cold up high ~ It is too noisy ~ It is too bright ~ It is too far
Q: What does the last line wish for? ~ To share the moon from afar ~ A long journey ~ Great wealth ~ A new home

@lesson id=c1-15 level=7 type=classic topic=Philosophy minutes=2 source=Analects of Confucius (public domain)
@title 论语·学而（节选）
@titleen Analects: On Learning (excerpt)
@summary Confucius on learning, friendship and not minding being unrecognised.
子曰：学而时习{习=to practise}之，不亦说[yue4]{说=to be pleased}乎？ // The Master said: To learn and then to practise it in due time, is that not a pleasure?
有朋自远方来，不亦乐乎？ // To have friends come from afar, is that not a joy?
人不知而不愠，不亦君子乎？ // To be unrecognised by others yet feel no resentment, is that not a gentleman?
Q: What does Confucius say is a pleasure? ~ Learning and practising ~ Winning ~ Travelling ~ Eating
Q: What brings joy? ~ Friends coming from afar ~ Gifts ~ Praise ~ Silence
Q: What is the mark of a gentleman? ~ Not resenting being unrecognised ~ Wealth ~ Long life ~ Strength

@lesson id=c1-16 level=7 type=classic topic=Philosophy minutes=2 source=Analects of Confucius (public domain)
@title 论语·为政（节选）
@titleen Analects: On Governing (excerpt)
@summary Short sayings about learning and thinking.
子曰：温故而知新，可以为[wei2]师矣。 // The Master said: One who revisits the old and learns the new is fit to be a teacher.
学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。 // Learning without thinking is confusing; thinking without learning is dangerous.
知之为[wei2]知之，不知为[wei2]不知，是知也。 // To know what you know and to know what you do not know: that is knowledge.
Q: What makes someone fit to be a teacher? ~ Revisiting the old and learning the new ~ Being old ~ Being rich ~ Writing books
Q: What happens if you learn without thinking? ~ You become confused ~ You become wise ~ You become tired ~ You forget
Q: What is true knowledge? ~ Knowing what you do and don't know ~ Knowing everything ~ Remembering books ~ Speaking well

@lesson id=c1-17 level=8 type=classic topic=Philosophy minutes=2 source=Daodejing (public domain)
@title 道德经·上善若水
@titleen Daodejing: The Highest Good Is Like Water
@summary Laozi praises water for benefiting all things without competing.
上善若水。 // The highest good is like water.
水善利万物而不争， // Water benefits all things and does not compete;
处众人之所恶，故几[ji1]{几=nearly}于道。 // it settles in places that people despise, and so comes close to the Way.
居善地，心善渊，与善仁，言善信， // In dwelling, be good at choosing the ground; in heart, be deep; in giving, be kind; in speech, be trustworthy;
政善治，事善能，动善时。 // in government, be orderly; in affairs, be capable; in action, be timely.
夫唯不争，故无尤。 // Only because it does not compete is it free from blame.
Q: What is the highest good compared with? ~ Water ~ Fire ~ Mountains ~ Wind
Q: What does water not do? ~ Compete ~ Flow ~ Help ~ Settle low
Q: Why is water free from blame? ~ Because it does not compete ~ Because it is clean ~ Because it is quiet ~ Because it is deep

@lesson id=c1-18 level=5 type=classic topic=Education minutes=2 source=Three Character Classic (public domain)
@title 三字经（开头）
@titleen Three Character Classic (opening)
@summary Traditional first lessons about human nature and education.
人之初，性本善。 // At the beginning, human nature is good.
性相近，习相远。 // By nature people are alike; through habit they grow apart.
苟不教，性乃迁。 // If they are not taught, their nature changes.
教之道，贵以专。 // The way of teaching values focus.
昔孟母，择邻处。 // Long ago Mencius's mother chose her neighbours carefully.
子{子=son}不学，断机杼。 // When her son would not study, she cut the cloth on her loom.
Q: According to the opening, human nature at birth is ... ~ Good ~ Bad ~ Neither ~ Unknown
Q: What does teaching value? ~ Focus ~ Speed ~ Strictness ~ Money
Q: What did Mencius's mother cut? ~ The cloth on her loom ~ Her hair ~ His books ~ A rope

@lesson id=c1-19 level=8 type=classic topic=Education minutes=2 source=Xunzi (public domain)
@title 劝学（节选）
@titleen An Exhortation to Learning (excerpt)
@summary Xunzi on how steady effort accomplishes great things.
青{青=blue-green dye}，取之于蓝，而青{青=bluer}于蓝； // Blue is taken from the indigo plant, yet is bluer than indigo;
冰，水为之，而寒于水。 // ice is made of water, yet is colder than water.
不积跬步，无以至千里； // Without accumulating small steps, you cannot reach a thousand miles;
不积小流，无以成江海。 // without gathering small streams, you cannot form rivers and seas.
锲而舍之，朽木不折； // If you give up carving, even rotten wood cannot be snapped;
锲而不舍，金石可镂。 // if you keep carving, even metal and stone can be engraved.
Q: What does 青出于蓝 suggest? ~ A student can surpass the teacher ~ Blue is rare ~ Indigo is cheap ~ Ice is blue
Q: What is needed to travel a thousand miles? ~ Accumulated small steps ~ A fast horse ~ A map ~ Good weather
Q: What can persistence engrave? ~ Metal and stone ~ Water ~ Paper ~ Wind

@lesson id=c1-20 level=8 type=classic topic=Literature minutes=2 source=Liu Yuxi (public domain)
@title 陋室铭（节选）
@titleen Inscription for a Humble Room (excerpt)
@summary Liu Yuxi finds dignity in a plain study.
山不在高，有仙则名{名=famous}。 // A mountain need not be high; with an immortal it becomes famous.
水不在深，有龙则灵{灵=divine}。 // Water need not be deep; with a dragon it becomes divine.
斯是陋室，惟吾德馨。 // This is a humble room, but my virtue makes it fragrant.
苔痕上阶绿，草色入帘青。 // Moss traces climb the steps in green; grass colour enters the blinds in blue.
谈笑有鸿儒，往来无白丁。 // Great scholars laugh and talk here; no commoners come and go.
Q: What makes a mountain famous? ~ An immortal ~ Its height ~ Its trees ~ Its snow
Q: How does the poet describe his room? ~ Humble but virtuous ~ Grand ~ Cold ~ Empty
Q: What colour is the moss? ~ Green ~ Grey ~ Yellow ~ Brown

@lesson id=c1-21 level=6 type=poem topic=Poetry minutes=1 source=Han Yuefu folk song (public domain)
@title 江南
@titleen South of the River
@summary A cheerful Han dynasty folk song about fish playing among lotus leaves.

鱼戏莲叶间。 // Fish play among the lotus leaves.
鱼戏莲叶东，鱼戏莲叶西， // Fish play east of the leaves, fish play west of the leaves,
鱼戏莲叶南，鱼戏莲叶北。 // fish play south of the leaves, fish play north of the leaves.
Q: What can be picked in the south? ~ Lotus ~ Tea ~ Rice ~ Cotton
Q: What are the fish doing? ~ Playing among the leaves ~ Sleeping ~ Hiding ~ Fighting
Q: Which directions are mentioned? ~ East, west, south and north ~ Only east ~ Up and down ~ Left and right
