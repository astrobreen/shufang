@summary Good manners when you visit a Chinese family.
@speakers 小王 玛丽
小王: 欢迎欢迎！快请进，不用换鞋。 // Welcome, welcome! Please come in. No need to take your shoes off.
玛丽: 谢谢。这是我给你们带的一点儿水果。 // Thank you. Here is some fruit I brought for you.
小王: 你太客气了，来就来嘛，还带什么东西！ // You're too polite. Just coming would be fine. Why bring anything?
玛丽: 在我们国家，去别人家做客，一定要带礼物。 // In my country, you must bring a gift when you visit someone's home.
小王: 在中国也是这样。来，先喝点儿茶。 // It's the same in China. Come on, have some tea first.
玛丽: 你们家真漂亮！ // Your home is beautiful!
小王: 哪里哪里，很小的。饭马上就好，请再等一会儿。 // Not at all, it's very small. Dinner will be ready soon. Please wait a little longer.
玛丽: 我能帮你做点儿什么吗？ // Can I help you with anything?
小王: 不用，你是客人，坐着聊天儿就行。 // No, you are a guest. Just sit and chat.
G: 哪里哪里 ~ 哪里哪里 is a modest reply to a compliment. ~ A: 你汉语说得真好！ B: 哪里哪里。 ~ A: You speak Chinese really well! B: Not at all. ~ 哪里哪里
G: 来就来嘛 ~ Verb + 就 + Verb (+ 嘛) means "just do it, that is enough"; a remark that nothing more is needed often follows. ~ 来就来，别带东西。 ~ Just come; don't bring anything. ~ 来
