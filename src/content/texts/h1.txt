@lesson id=h1-01 level=1 type=article topic=Daily life minutes=2 source=Original
@title 我叫王明
@titleen My Name Is Wang Ming
@summary A boy introduces himself, his age and his city.
我叫王明。 // My name is Wang Ming.
我是中国人。 // I am Chinese.
我今年十二岁。 // I am twelve years old this year.
我住在北京。 // I live in Beijing.
我是学生，我在北京一中学习。 // I am a student, and I study at Beijing No. 1 Middle School.
我的老师姓李。 // My teacher's surname is Li.
我喜欢我的学校，也喜欢我的老师。 // I like my school, and I like my teacher too.
你叫什么名字？ // What is your name?
G: 我是…… ~ 是 (shì) means "to be". Use it to say who or what someone is. ~ 我是学生。 ~ I am a student. ~ 是
G: 我住在…… ~ 住在 + place tells where you live. ~ 我住在上海。 ~ I live in Shanghai. ~ 在
Q: How old is Wang Ming? ~ Twelve ~ Ten ~ Fifteen ~ Twenty
Q: Where does Wang Ming live? ~ Beijing ~ Shanghai ~ Chengdu ~ Hong Kong
Q: What is his teacher's surname? ~ Li ~ Wang ~ Zhang ~ Chen

@lesson id=h1-02 level=1 type=dialogue topic=Time minutes=2 source=Original
@title 现在几点了？
@titleen What Time Is It Now?
@summary Two friends check the time before school.
@speakers 小明 小红
小明: 小红，现在几点了？ // Xiaohong, what time is it now?
小红: 现在七点半。 // It is half past seven now.
小明: 啊！八点上课，我们快走吧。 // Oh! Class starts at eight. Let's hurry.
小红: 不要急，学校很近。 // Don't worry, the school is very close.
小明: 你吃早饭了吗？ // Did you eat breakfast?
小红: 吃了。我吃了一个鸡蛋和一个面包。 // Yes. I ate an egg and a piece of bread.
小明: 我没吃。我现在很饿。 // I didn't eat. I'm very hungry now.
小红: 我有一个苹果，给你。 // I have an apple. Here you go.
小明: 谢谢你！ // Thank you!
G: 几点 ~ Ask the time with 几点 (what hour). Answer with number + 点. ~ 现在几点？ ~ What time is it now?  ~ 几
G: 了 after a verb ~ 了 after a verb shows the action is finished. ~ 我吃了早饭。 ~ I ate breakfast. ~ 了
Q: What time is it now? ~ 7:30 ~ 8:00 ~ 7:00 ~ 8:30
Q: What did Xiaohong eat for breakfast? ~ An egg and bread ~ Noodles ~ Rice and soup ~ Nothing
Q: What does Xiaohong give Xiaoming? ~ An apple ~ An egg ~ Some bread ~ A book

@lesson id=h1-03 level=1 type=dialogue topic=Shopping minutes=2 source=Original
@title 买苹果
@titleen Buying Apples
@summary A customer buys apples and asks about the price.
@speakers 顾客 老板
顾客: 老板，苹果怎么卖？ // Boss, how much are the apples?
老板: 五块钱一斤。 // Five yuan per jin.
顾客: 太贵了。三块钱，可以吗？ // Too expensive. Three yuan, okay?
老板: 不行不行。四块钱吧。 // No, no. Make it four yuan.
顾客: 好吧。我要两斤。 // All right. I want two jin.
老板: 一共八块钱。 // Eight yuan in total.
顾客: 给你十块钱。 // Here is ten yuan.
老板: 找你两块钱。再见！ // Here's two yuan change. Goodbye!
顾客: 再见！ // Goodbye!
G: 太……了 ~ 太 + adjective + 了 means "too ..." or "so ...". ~ 这个太大了。 ~ This one is too big. ~ 太
G: 一共 ~ 一共 means "altogether" and is used when you add up numbers. ~ 一共多少钱？ ~ How much is it altogether? ~ 一共
Q: How much does one jin of apples cost at first? ~ 5 yuan ~ 3 yuan ~ 4 yuan ~ 8 yuan
Q: How many jin does the customer buy? ~ Two ~ One ~ Three ~ Five
Q: How much change does the customer get? ~ 2 yuan ~ 1 yuan ~ 5 yuan ~ None

@lesson id=h1-04 level=1 type=dialogue topic=Weather minutes=2 source=Original
@title 今天天气怎么样？
@titleen How Is the Weather Today?
@summary A mother and her son decide what to wear.
@speakers 妈妈 小明
妈妈: 小明，你看，外面在下雨。 // Xiaoming, look. It is raining outside.
小明: 真的吗？昨天天气很好。 // Really? The weather was very good yesterday.
妈妈: 今天很冷，你要多穿一点儿。 // It is very cold today. You should wear a bit more.
小明: 我不冷，我不想穿这件衣服。 // I'm not cold. I don't want to wear this piece of clothing.
妈妈: 不行，你会生病的。 // No. You will get sick.
小明: 好吧。我能带我的新雨伞吗？ // Fine. Can I take my new umbrella?
妈妈: 当然可以。明天天气会好吗？ // Of course. Will the weather be good tomorrow?
小明: 不知道。电视上说明天会有太阳。 // I don't know. The TV says it will be sunny tomorrow.
G: 在 + verb ~ 在 before a verb means the action is going on right now. ~ 他在看书。 ~ He is reading. ~ 在
G: 会 for the future ~ 会 can also mean "will" when you guess what is going to happen. ~ 明天会下雨。 ~ It will rain tomorrow. ~ 会
Q: What is the weather like today? ~ Rainy and cold ~ Sunny and hot ~ Windy and warm ~ Snowy
Q: What does Xiaoming want to take? ~ A new umbrella ~ A coat ~ A hat ~ A book
Q: What does the TV say about tomorrow? ~ It will be sunny ~ It will rain ~ It will snow ~ It will be cold

@lesson id=h1-06 level=1 type=dialogue topic=Food minutes=3 source=Original
@title 你想吃什么？
@titleen What Would You Like to Eat?
@summary Two people order lunch in a restaurant.
@speakers 服务员 马克
服务员: 你好，请坐。你们想吃什么？ // Hello, please sit down. What would you like to eat?
马克: 我想吃米饭和鸡肉。 // I would like rice and chicken.
服务员: 你要喝什么？ // What would you like to drink?
马克: 我要一杯茶。我朋友要一杯水。 // I'll have a cup of tea. My friend will have a glass of water.
服务员: 好的，请等一下。 // All right, please wait a moment.
马克: 对不起，这个菜我没点。 // Sorry, I didn't order this dish.
服务员: 啊，不好意思，我马上拿走。 // Oh, I'm sorry. I'll take it away right away.
马克: 没关系。菜很好吃，谢谢！ // It's okay. The food is delicious, thank you!
G: 想 + verb ~ 想 before a verb means "would like to" or "want to". ~ 我想喝茶。 ~ I would like to drink tea. ~ 想
G: 对不起 / 没关系 ~ Say 对不起 to say sorry, and 没关系 to answer "it doesn't matter". ~ 对不起，我来晚了。 ~ Sorry, I am late. ~ 对不起
Q: What does Mark want to eat? ~ Rice and chicken ~ Noodles and beef ~ Fish and soup ~ Only a salad
Q: What does Mark's friend drink? ~ Water ~ Tea ~ Coffee ~ Milk
Q: What was wrong with the dish? ~ Mark did not order it ~ It was cold ~ It was too salty ~ It was too small

@lesson id=h1-08 level=1 type=dialogue topic=Family minutes=2 source=Original
@title 这是谁？
@titleen Who Is This?
@summary A girl shows a family photo to her friend.
@speakers 小美 大卫
大卫: 小美，这是谁？ // Xiaomei, who is this?
小美: 这是我爸爸，这是我妈妈。 // This is my father, and this is my mother.
大卫: 这个小孩儿是谁？ // Who is this little child?
小美: 他是我弟弟，他今年五岁。 // He is my younger brother. He is five years old this year.
大卫: 你家有几口人？ // How many people are there in your family?
小美: 有五口人：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、弟弟和我。 // Five: father, mother, older brother, younger brother, and me.
大卫: 你哥哥在哪儿工作？ // Where does your older brother work?
小美: 他在医院工作，他是医生。 // He works at a hospital. He is a doctor.
G: 几口人 ~ 家有几口人？ asks how many people are in a family. 口 is the measure word for family members. ~ 我家有四口人。 ~ There are four people in my family. ~ 几
G: 在 + place + verb ~ 在 + place goes before the verb to say where something happens. ~ 他在家看书。 ~ He reads at home. ~ 在
Q: How old is Xiaomei's younger brother? ~ Five ~ Three ~ Eight ~ Ten
Q: How many people are in Xiaomei's family? ~ Five ~ Four ~ Six ~ Three
Q: What does Xiaomei's older brother do? ~ He is a doctor ~ He is a teacher ~ He is a student ~ He is a driver

@lesson id=h1-10 level=1 type=story topic=Animals minutes=3 source=Original
@title 小狗找骨头
@titleen The Little Dog Looks for a Bone
@summary A little dog cannot find his bone, until a bird helps him.
小狗有一根骨头。 // A little dog had a bone.
他很喜欢这根骨头，不想给别人吃。 // He liked this bone very much and did not want to give it to anyone.
下午，他把骨头放在树下，然后去喝水。 // In the afternoon he put the bone under a tree, and then went to drink water.
他回来的时候，骨头不见了！ // When he came back, the bone was gone!
小狗很难过，他到处找，可是找不到。 // The little dog was very sad. He searched everywhere but could not find it.
这时，一只小鸟说："我看到了，小猫把它拿走了。" // At that moment a little bird said, "I saw it. The kitten took it away."
小狗去找小猫。小猫说："对不起，我以为这是我的。" // The little dog went to find the kitten. The kitten said, "I'm sorry. I thought it was mine."
小猫把骨头还给了小狗，他们成了好朋友。 // The kitten gave the bone back to the little dog, and they became good friends.
G: 找不到 ~ Verb + 不 + result means "cannot do it". ~ 我看不到你。 ~ I cannot see you. ~ 不
G: 把 ~ 把 moves the object before the verb: it tells what is done to the thing. ~ 我把书放在桌子上。 ~ I put the book on the table. ~ 把
Q: Where did the dog put the bone? ~ Under a tree ~ In the house ~ On the table ~ In the river
Q: Who saw what happened? ~ A little bird ~ A cat ~ A duck ~ A horse
Q: How does the story end? ~ They become friends ~ The dog is angry ~ The bone is lost ~ The cat runs away
